s.tier.curator – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

s.tier.curator ("the app") is a personal publishing tool operated by the owner of the Instagram and TikTok account @s.tier.curator. It is used only by its owner to prepare and send the owner's own content (image carousels and slideshow videos about classic cars and watches) to the owner's own TikTok account.

What the app accesses

After the owner authorizes it through TikTok's login, the app can access basic information about the owner's own TikTok account and can upload or publish content to that account. It does not access any other user's account.

What the app stores

The app stores only the authorization token needed to post to the owner's account, plus the content the owner chooses to publish. It does not collect personal data from viewers, followers, or any other TikTok users.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties. Data is passed only to TikTok, as required to publish the owner's content.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services. Stored tokens can then be deleted on request.

Changes

This policy may be updated; the effective date above will change when it is.

Contact

Questions: send a direct message to @s.tier.curator on Instagram.
